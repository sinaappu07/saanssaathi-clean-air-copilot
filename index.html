Red Alert Smog Protocol

60-Second Winter Smog Defense

1. Seal Window Gaps: Pack rolled damp towels firmly along all exterior window sills.
2. Run Bedroom CADR at Maximum: Position air cleaner 1.5m from breathing zone.
3. Follow each person’s existing care plan. For urgent breathing symptoms, contact a qualified health professional or local emergency service.
4. Zero Frying & Broom Sweeping: Wet mop floors only to prevent soot aerosolization.
